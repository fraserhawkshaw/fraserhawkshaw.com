What I do

I started in 2022 filming socials in nightclubs, editing in the club so the clips went up the same night.

When multi-cam took off in the music scene, I brought that same speed to it: footage from several cameras, cut into finished clips within hours and out on socials by the next morning. That's still how I work.

Now I direct and vision mix live streams and IMAG for festival screens, record full sets, and cut the highlights. I'll also handle the prep, from recce and camera plan to crew, kit and travel, so you're dealing with one person from start to finish. If you've already got a director, you can book me as a camera operator.

On the side I built Confirmation Desk, an app that writes booking confirmations for freelancers, after one too many jobs where nothing was agreed in writing.

Booking something? Send the date, venue, set times and what you need, and I'll get back to you.
